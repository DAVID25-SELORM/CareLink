# Pharmacist guide

## Dispensing
1. **Pharmacy:** open the pending prescriptions. Only **paid** prescriptions can be dispensed. If it is not paid, send the patient to the cashier.
2. Check the patient, drug, dose and allergies on the prescription.
3. **Dispense.** Stock comes from the batch that expires first. If a batch is expired the system will not dispense it. If stock is near expiry you are asked to confirm.

## Stock
- **Batches** tab: when a delivery arrives, add the drug, **batch number, quantity and expiry date**. Never leave expiry blank.
- Read the banner at the top: it lists expired and soon-to-expire stock. Remove expired stock from the shelf the same day.
- **Drugs** and **Procurement:** keep the catalogue and orders up to date.

## Never
- Dispense an expired or unpaid prescription by overriding the screen. If something looks wrong, ask the doctor or administrator.
- Hand out medicine and fix the record later.
