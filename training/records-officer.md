# Records officer guide

## Registering a patient
1. **Patients:** search by name, phone or NHIS number first. Look carefully at date of birth and address before deciding it is a new patient.
2. **Register New Patient:** fill in the details. Enter the **real date of birth** where known.
3. **Consent:** read the privacy notice to the patient (or guardian for a child). Tick treatment and data-processing consent only if they agree. Tick **Agrees to SMS reminders** only if they want texts, and confirm the phone number is theirs (or the guardian's for a child).
4. Put the patient on the **queue** and give them the ticket.

## Records
- **Records:** look up and correct details. Corrections are recorded.
- **NHIS CC Code:** record the claim check code for insured patients.
- Duplicate found? Tell the administrator. Do not delete anything.

## Never
- Register a patient without consent being asked.
- Change a patient's details because someone phoned and asked; check who they are first.
