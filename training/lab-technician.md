# Laboratory guide

1. **Laboratory:** open the requested tests. Check the patient and the sample label.
2. **Sample Tracking:** record when each sample is received, processed and finished.
3. Enter results with the **correct unit**. The screen compares against reference ranges for the patient's **age and sex**, and flags results as normal, abnormal or **critical**.
4. A **critical** result must be telephoned to the requesting doctor straight away and recorded. Do not wait for the doctor to open the screen.
5. Check every result before saving, and tell the administrator if a saved result needs correcting.

## Notes
- Reference ranges and critical limits come from a built-in list that your laboratory lead must have signed off. If your laboratory uses different limits, tell the administrator before going live.
- Radiology results follow the same careful entry.

## Never
- Release a critical result only by SMS or note.
- Enter results from memory. Enter them from the instrument printout or worksheet.
