# Doctor guide

## A consultation
1. **Queue Management:** call the next patient, then **Start Encounter**.
2. Open the patient: check **allergies** first. Record any new allergy before prescribing.
3. Record the history, examination and **diagnosis** with the right ICD-10 code. Use the correct code for long-term conditions (hypertension, diabetes, asthma and similar): the Chronic Care list finds patients by that code.
4. **Prescriptions:** pick the drugs. The system warns about recorded allergies and serious drug interactions.
   - A warning is not a block. Read it. Confirm only if you decided it is safe; your confirmation is recorded.
5. **Laboratory:** request tests. Results flagged **critical** need your action the same day.
6. Complete the encounter.

## Other screens you may use
Appointments, Ward Rounds, Referrals, Telemedicine, Maternity, Theatre, Discharge, Chronic Care, Child Welfare.

## Never
- Ignore a critical lab result because the patient has left. Call the patient.
- Prescribe without checking allergies when the record says none "recorded": ask the patient.
- Tell a patient their results by SMS.
