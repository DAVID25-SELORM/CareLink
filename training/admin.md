# Administrator guide

You set the facility up and approve sensitive actions. Your menu has everything.

## First time
1. **Hospital Profile:** name, contact phone and email. Receipts and SMS use these.
2. **Users:** create one login for every staff member with the right role. No shared accounts. Remove access the day someone leaves.
3. **Drugs** and **Services & Fees:** load the catalogue and prices.
4. **Pharmacy > Batches** (with the pharmacist): enter opening stock with batch and expiry dates.
5. **Go-live Checklist:** work down it until every system item is ticked, and confirm the sign-offs with the right people.

## Every day
- **Cashier > Refund requests:** approve or decline refunds cashiers asked for. Approve only with a good reason and only after seeing the receipt. Declining needs a reason the cashier can read.
- **Cashier > Day close:** check each day was closed and that any cash difference has an explanation.
- **Pharmacy:** look at the expiry banner.

## Now and then
- **Patients > Export CSV:** a full copy of the register. It holds personal data: save it somewhere secure and delete copies you no longer need. Every export is recorded.
- **Notifications (SMS):** send a single text or look at the sent log.
- **Chronic Care** and **Child Welfare:** see who has missed follow-ups or vaccines.

## Never
- Share your admin login or let someone else approve refunds under it.
- Refund directly from the Completed tab when a cashier asked: use the Refund requests tab so the record is complete.
