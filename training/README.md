## Rules for everyone
1. **One person, one login.** Never share a password or stay signed in on a shared computer. Sign out when you leave.
2. **Search before you register.** A patient registered twice splits their records, which is a safety risk.
3. **Never put diagnoses, results or medicine names in an SMS.** Texts can be read by anyone holding the phone.
4. **If the system is down,** use the paper procedure your facility agreed, and enter the records afterwards, marked with the real date.
5. **If something looks wrong** (a wrong total, a patient with someone else's details), stop and tell the administrator. Do not try to fix it by registering again or paying again.
6. These guides describe the screens as built; menu names can differ slightly. If a screen does not match, tell the administrator.
