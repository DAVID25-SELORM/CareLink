# Cashier guide

## Taking a payment
1. **Cashier > Pending Bills:** find the patient and open the bill. Check the name and total with the patient.
2. Choose how they pay.
   - **Cash:** collect, then save.
   - **Mobile Money:** choose the network (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo). Look at the **confirmation SMS on the facility's phone or wallet** to confirm the money has arrived. Type the **transaction ID** exactly. The system does not check it automatically. A reference already used on another payment is refused.
   - **Card:** type the receipt number from the card machine.
   - **Insurance:** for NHIS patients.
3. Save, then **Print** or **Download** the receipt and give it to the patient. The prescription now goes to the pharmacy.

## Refunds
You cannot refund directly. On **Completed**, click **Request refund** and give the reason. An administrator approves or declines it. Look at the **Refund requests** tab to see the answer. A refund is only possible while the medicine has not been dispensed.

## End of day
1. **Day close:** the screen shows what you should have taken, by method.
2. Count the cash in the till and type the amount.
3. If it differs from the expected amount, write why (for example, change given). You cannot close with an unexplained difference.
4. Check mobile money against the wallet statement and card against the machine report.

## Never
- Save a mobile money payment before you have seen the confirmation message.
- Hand back cash for a refund that is not approved.
- Share your login so someone else can take payments under your name.
