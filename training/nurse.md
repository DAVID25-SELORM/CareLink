# Nurse guide

## Receiving a patient
1. **Patients:** search first (name, phone or NHIS number). Register only if there is no match.
2. Put the patient on the **Queue** (OPD or another department). **Ticket** prints a number with the first name only.
3. **Triage:** record vital signs. In an emergency use Triage and mark priority.
4. On the patient's page you can **Print wristband** (name, ID, age and any recorded allergies). Use it for admissions and anyone being treated.

## Child welfare (clinic days)
1. **Child Welfare:** pick the child. If there is no birth date, enter the real one from the child health card.
2. The table shows each vaccine as given, due, overdue or past the age limit. **Record dose** with the date, batch number and injection site. Each vaccine is recorded once.
3. Record the weight. A red alert means the weight dropped since the last visit: assess and refer.
4. **Overdue list:** children under 5 with missed vaccines. **Send SMS** asks the guardian to bring the child in; it does not name vaccines. It only works if the guardian agreed to SMS on the child's record.

## Chronic care
**Chronic Care** lists patients with long-term conditions who have not visited recently. Phone them or use **Send SMS** (no condition is named).

## Never
- Register someone twice.
- Record a dose that was not given, or a vaccine you did not see given.
- Put a vaccine name or illness in a text message.
